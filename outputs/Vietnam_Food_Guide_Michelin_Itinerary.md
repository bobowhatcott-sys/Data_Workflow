# Vietnam Food Guide (Itinerary-Based)

**Travel dates:** May 3–13  
**Cities:** Hoi An, Da Nang, Ninh Binh, Hanoi  
**Focus:** Michelin-recognized restaurants + strongly reviewed local spots + first-time must-try dishes

---

## Quick Booking Priorities
- Book now: Gia (Hanoi), Hibana by Koki (Hanoi), La Maison 1888 (Da Nang), Nén Danang.
- Book 1–2 days ahead: Bib Gourmand places in Hanoi/Da Nang.
- Walk-in friendly: Street food and local breakfast noodle shops.

## Hoi An / Da Nang (May 4–7)
### Michelin & top picks
1. La Maison 1888 (Da Nang) — 1 Michelin Star (fine-dining splurge)
2. Nén Danang (Da Nang) — Michelin Green Star (ingredient-focused tasting)
3. Bánh Xèo 76 (Da Nang) — Bib Gourmand
4. Bún Bò Huế Bà Thương (Da Nang) — Bib Gourmand
5. Quê Xưa (Da Nang) — Bib Gourmand
6. Morning Glory Original (Hoi An) — iconic, highly reviewed first-timer spot
### First-time foods to try here
- Cao lầu (Hoi An)
- White rose dumplings (bánh bao bánh vạc)
- Mì Quảng
- Bánh xèo (central style)
- Bún bò Huế

## Ninh Binh (May 8)
### Local specialties (best use of this stop)
- Cơm cháy Ninh Bình (crispy rice)
- Dê núi (mountain goat dishes)
- Ốc núi (mountain snails, seasonal)

## Hanoi (May 10–12)
### Michelin star priorities
1. Gia — 1 Michelin Star (modern Vietnamese tasting menu)
2. Tầm Vị — 1 Michelin Star (classic northern Vietnamese)
3. Hibana by Koki — 1 Michelin Star (luxury teppanyaki)
### Michelin value picks (Bib Gourmand)
- Miến Lươn Chân Cầm
- Phở Bò Lâm
- Bún Chả Chan
### First-time foods to try in Hanoi
- Phở bò
- Bún chả
- Chả cá
- Miến lươn
- Egg coffee (cà phê trứng)

## Suggested Meal Plan by Day
- May 4 (late arrival): light Hoi An dinner — cao lầu + white rose dumplings
- May 5: Morning Glory Original lunch; local seafood or Mì Quảng for dinner
- May 6: flexible tasting day (bánh mì, bánh xèo, chè)
- May 7: Da Nang lunch at Bib Gourmand spot; optional La Maison 1888 dinner
- May 8: Ninh Binh goat + cơm cháy lunch
- May 10: Hanoi dinner at Tầm Vị
- May 11: Bib lunch + Gia dinner
- May 12: bún chả + egg coffee lunch; optional Hibana by Koki final dinner

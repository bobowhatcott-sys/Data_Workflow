# 🇻🇳 Vietnam Food Guide (Itinerary-Based)

> **Travel dates:** May 3–13  
> **Cities:** Hoi An · Da Nang · Ninh Binh · Hanoi  
> **Focus:** Michelin highlights, top-reviewed local spots, and first-time must-try dishes

---

## ✅ Quick Booking Priorities

- **Book now:** Gia (Hanoi), Hibana by Koki (Hanoi), La Maison 1888 (Da Nang), Nén Danang.
- **Book 1–2 days ahead:** Bib Gourmand picks in Hanoi and Da Nang.
- **Walk-in friendly:** Street food, noodle shops, and most breakfast spots.

## 🌊 Hoi An / Da Nang (May 4–7)

### Michelin & Top Picks
| Priority | Restaurant | Why Go |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **La Maison 1888 (Da Nang)** | 1 Michelin Star, destination fine dining |
| 2 | **Nén Danang (Da Nang)** | Michelin Green Star, ingredient-led tasting menu |
| 3 | **Bánh Xèo 76 (Da Nang)** | Bib Gourmand, classic central bánh xèo |
| 4 | **Bún Bò Huế Bà Thương (Da Nang)** | Bib Gourmand, rich spicy noodle soup |
| 5 | **Quê Xưa (Da Nang)** | Bib Gourmand, strong local flavors |
| 6 | **Morning Glory Original (Hoi An)** | Popular first-timer spot in Hoi An |

### First-Time Foods to Try
- Cao lầu (Hoi An)
- White rose dumplings (*bánh bao bánh vạc*)
- Mì Quảng
- Bánh xèo (central style)
- Bún bò Huế

## 🏞️ Ninh Binh (May 8)

### Local Specialty Focus
- Cơm cháy Ninh Bình (crispy rice)
- Dê núi (mountain goat dishes)
- Ốc núi (mountain snails, seasonal)

## 🏙️ Hanoi (May 10–12)

### Michelin Star Priorities
1. **Gia** — 1 Michelin Star (modern Vietnamese tasting menu)
2. **Tầm Vị** — 1 Michelin Star (classic northern Vietnamese)
3. **Hibana by Koki** — 1 Michelin Star (luxury teppanyaki)

### Michelin Value Picks (Bib Gourmand)
- Miến Lươn Chân Cầm
- Phở Bò Lâm
- Bún Chả Chan

### First-Time Foods to Try
- Phở bò
- Bún chả
- Chả cá
- Miến lươn
- Egg coffee (*cà phê trứng*)

---

## 🍽️ Suggested Meal Plan by Day

- **May 4 (late arrival):** Hoi An light dinner — cao lầu + white rose dumplings
- **May 5:** Morning Glory lunch; local seafood or Mì Quảng dinner
- **May 6:** Flexible tasting day (bánh mì, bánh xèo, chè)
- **May 7:** Da Nang Bib Gourmand lunch; optional La Maison 1888 dinner
- **May 8:** Ninh Binh goat + cơm cháy lunch
- **May 10:** Hanoi dinner at Tầm Vị
- **May 11:** Bib lunch + Gia dinner
- **May 12:** Bún chả + egg coffee lunch; optional Hibana by Koki dinner
